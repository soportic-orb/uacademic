# Teacher guide

What you need to know to use UAcademic. Five minutes of reading.

---

## Signing in

With your university account — the same one as your email. There is no new
password to remember.

If you hold posts in more than one center, the selector in the header says
which one you are looking at.

---

## Install it on your phone

It is worth doing, and on an iPhone it is the only way to get notifications.

- **Android or desktop**: the browser offers to install it; there is also a
  button on the dashboard.
- **iPhone and iPad**: Safari → **Share** button → **Add to Home Screen**. On
  iOS notifications only arrive once the app is installed this way.

Once installed, your timetable can be read **offline**. On a train with no
signal you see the last saved version, with a note saying so.

---

## Your timetable

**Calendar** has four views: day, week, month and list. The week is the one
everybody uses.

You can take it into the calendar you already use, under **Connections**:

- **Subscription (ICS)**: works everywhere. It is slow: Google reads it every
  8-24 hours and there is no way to speed that up. Not useful for a change
  today.
- **Microsoft or Google**: changes appear immediately. We write into a calendar
  of our own, never into your personal one, and if you delete a class from your
  phone we put it back at the next sync — UAcademic is the source of truth.

If you switch on reverse sync, we read only the hours you are busy. Never the
titles or the contents of your events.

## Your teaching load

**My load** compares the hours you are contracted for with the hours you are
assigned. The traffic light is the same everywhere:

| Colour | Means                       |
| ------ | --------------------------- |
| Blue   | Below your capacity         |
| Green  | In the optimal band         |
| Amber  | At the limit                |
| Red    | Above what you should carry |

It always carries an icon and a label, never colour alone.

---

## Availability

In your profile you can say which slots you prefer to teach in, which you can
take, which you would rather avoid and which are impossible. Planning takes
this into account: impossible is a block, "rather avoid" carries a cost.

For a particular day — a medical appointment, a conference — use an
**exception**, with the date. The reason you write is seen only by whoever
coordinates, and is never sent to the AI.

---

## Changing a class

From the class, **Propose a change**. The route is:

1. You ask, and pick the new slot or the person covering for you.
2. Whoever is involved accepts.
3. Coordination approves, if approval is binding in your center.
4. It is written into the timetable and everybody affected is told.

If the new slot has a conflict — an overlap, a room that is too small, more
consecutive hours than allowed — you are told before anything is requested, and
told why.

---

## Messages and notifications

**Messages** are internal conversations: per subject, per group, or direct.

**Notifications** arrive by email and, if you turned them on, on your phone. In
your profile you choose what to receive through each channel. A few cannot be
turned off — a class of yours being moved, for instance — because they change
your working day.

---

## Documents

The center's repository: regulations, guides and teaching plans that may apply
to you. Search by title and filter by type. If a document has expired, it says
so.

---

## Your data

Under **Privacy** you can download everything the platform holds about you, as
one file. You can also ask for your account to be erased: the center's
administration carries it out, and the page explains what goes and what stays —
classes taught and the audit trail stay, because a center has to be able to say
who did what, but you remain there as an anonymous account.

The AI assistant is a coordination tool: it sends Anthropic the questions, the
center's regulations and organisational data with names and internal
identifiers. Never an identity document, phone number, address, bank details or
anything medical. And it writes nothing without a person confirming it.

---

## Things that happen

**My timetable is not there.** Check that the year is published: while it is a
draft, nobody sees it.

**Notifications do not reach my phone.** On iOS the app has to be on the home
screen. Everywhere, you have to have granted permission when it was asked for.

**I can see another center's data.** That should not happen: switch center in
the header selector, and if it continues, tell the administration.
